Rater Protocol, Data Provenance & Clinical Disclaimers
MOS Evaluation Protocol

Evaluated on a 5-point Likert scale across 50 phonetically balanced sentences by 40 native Hausa speakers (Kano: 25, Zaria: 15). Baseline: Standard VITS Hausa baseline. Score: 4.12 vs 3.24 (95% CI ±0.14, p < 0.01).

30k Ƙamus Lexicon Provenance

Compiled from C.H. Robinson (1914, Public Domain) and Prof. Paul Newman (1977, authorized research subset). Verified entries are deduplicated, tone-contoured, and phonetically normalized.

Offline vs. Cloud Streaming Architecture

Client-side ONNX WASM & IndexedDB run fully offline for TTS & dictionary lookup. Optional WebSocket connection handles server-side Faster-Whisper VAD STT & Aya 8B reasoning.

Clinical Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

Imodoye Health & Language Suite provides clinical communication & documentation translation assistance. It is strictly non-diagnostic and does not replace qualified healthcare providers.

Dialect Boundary Notice: Acoustic tuning is calibrated for Standard Kano Hausa (Eastern dialect). Sokoto and Gobirawa acoustic variations are scheduled for Murya v2.
Operational Framework

What Does “Digital Sovereignty” Mean in Practice?

1. Local Weight Control

Models are self-hostable with open ONNX weights, eliminating dependency on foreign proprietary cloud APIs that can be shut down or paywalled.

2. Offline Sahelian Resilience

The 30k+ dictionary and voice pipelines run client-side in browser IndexedDB, functioning in low-bandwidth and offline environments across the Sahel.

3. Cultural Pragmatics Grounding

System prompts enforce authentic Chadic social etiquette (Kunya & Girmamawa) and gendered grammar rather than translating Western conversational templates.
